## Design goals

- An integrated mobile digital experience that provides users with a deeper understanding of diabetes through accessible health insights, educational resources, and personalized information.
- Empower individuals to better understand their condition and make more informed decisions about their health.
- Reimagine the way people interact with their health
- A concept showcasing technology, interaction and design capabilities of the future
