## Design goals

- Reimagine the Waste Management native (iOS / Android) mobile experience that simplifies everyday tasks, streamlines service management, and delivers seamless interactions across the customer journey.
- The new mobile companion will keep customers informed and connected through proactive holiday and service notifications, real-time pickup tracking, and timely service updates—providing greater visibility, convenience, and control over their waste management services.
- In addition, an educational experience to empower customers with recycling resources, sustainability tips, and information about green initiatives, encouraging environmentally responsible behaviors and greater community engagement.
