## Design goals

- Reimagine the JCPenney mobile app with a modern, streamlined product discovery, simplified navigation and enhanced shopping experience.
- A new native experience that modernizes current shopping experience and elevates JCPenney’s brand
- Use the mobile platform to create a memorable experience.
- Integrated digital wallet, mobile payment, and other native mobile capabilities to simplify checkout, reduce purchasing friction, and create a faster shopping experience.
