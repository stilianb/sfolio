## Overview

Experian is a global data and technology company operating across consumer credit, financial services, identity, and business intelligence. With a broad portfolio of products, services, and digital experiences.

Experian needed a consistent digital experience across its digital ecosystem. Establish a unified, scalable design system that brings the Experian brand to life across every digital touchpoint.

## Challenges

- As digital products and services evolve, maintaining a consistent experience across multiple platforms, teams, and user journeys became increasingly complex.
- Disconnected design and development workflows: Inconsistent processes and duplicated efforts created a confusing user experience
- The absence of a universal design language made it challenging to maintain consistency as products and teams evlolved.

## Design goals

- Create a design system that defines Experian's visual language, design principles, component library, and interaction patterns. Establish a single source of truth for design and engineering teams.
- Develop a library of reusable, modular UI components, design tokens, patterns, and guidelines that support different products, platforms, and business needs.
- Translate complex financial information, analytics, and data-heavy workflows into clear, intuitive, and accessible interfaces that help users make confident decisions.
- Align design, product, engineering, and brand teams around shared standards, documentation, and governance to improve collaboration and accelerate delivery.

## Impact

- Delivered a unified design system that created a stronger foundation for Experian to scale while maintaining consistency, quality, and customer trust.
- Reduced duplicated efforts and helped teams design, build and deploy faster.
- Strengthen Experian's brand across all products, platforms, and digital experiences.
- Improved accessibility across all products
- Created alignment across design, engineering, and business teams
- Established a scalable and flexible foundation to grow with technologies, new products, and customer expectations.
