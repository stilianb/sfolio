## Overview

Create a modern, scalable design system that establishes a common digital language across Cisco.com, unifying experiences across enterprise products, services, and business units.

Evolve Cisco’s digital experience to reflect its transformation from a global hardware leader into a modern, SaaS technology company.

## Challenges

- An aging web experience with complex content architecture and unintuitive navigation, making it difficult for users to find relevant information.
- Inconsistent SaaS messaging and commerce capabilities across products and services.
- Underdeveloped discovery tools, insufficient investment in search functionality, content discoverability, and social integration.
- Excessive industry jargon and promotional language that creates confusion rather than clearly communicating customer value

## Design goals

- Define and deliver a new and reimagined Cisco.com experience
- Reimagine Cisco.com by leveraging best-in-class SaaS experiences and modern design principles to set a new standard for customer engagement.
- Communicate Cisco's brand promise, through new digital experience, identity, brand architecture, personalization and personalized experience for customers, partners, and employee.
- Transform navigation, content architecture, and UI patterns into an intuitive, accessible, and consistent experience across the Cisco digital ecosystem.

## Impact

The new <em>Cisco Reimagined</em> design system transformed Cisco's digital presence and strengthened its brand perception, built customer trust, and drove meaningful engagements.

Created a greater consistency across Cisco's entities, improved discoverability and overall accessibility. Made it easier for customers, partners, and employees to connect with Cisco's products, services, and solutions.

Elevated Cisco's brand perception and reinforce Cisco's position as a modern SaaS leader. Increased customer engagement and drove consistency through a shared design language. Strengthened customer relationships by delivering an experience based on user needs.
