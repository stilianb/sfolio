Overview

Partnered with the Workplace Services (WPS) team to design a unified native mobile and desktop experience for ServiceNow campus visitors, enabling them to easily navigate office locations, discover collaborative spaces, connect with colleagues, and access essential campus services.

Leveraged ServiceNow's Horizon native mobile platform, ODS Design System, and out-of-the-box (OOTB) capabilities to deliver a consistent, experience aligned with ServiceNow's established design standards while maximizing platform functionality and minimizing custom development.
