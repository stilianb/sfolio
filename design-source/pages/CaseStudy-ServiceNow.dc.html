ServiceNow

Project
MOA Redlining & Clause Library
Role
Sr. Staff UX/UI Lead
Type
Enterprise Web App
Scope
Discovery, UX Design, Visual Design 

Overview

Design and deliver new Clauses and Redlining experiences for MOA, Order Forms, and User Agreements, enabling users to collaborate seamlessly throughout the contract creation and negotiation process.

Leverage out-of-the-box capabilities to establish a consistent, intuitive experience across LCC, CMPro, and SURF—streamlining workflows, improving collaboration, and creating a more unified contract lifecycle experience.

Discovery

  • Led and facilitated workshops with business stakeholders, engineering, and cross-platform teams to align on objectives, define priorities, and establish a shared vision.
  • Partnered with user research team to conduct a global assessment of existing CMPro, LCC, and SURF capabilities, translating user insights and feedback into actionable items, requirements, and user stories.

  • Collaborated with engineering teams through focused workshops to evaluate out-of-the-box (OOTB) capabilities, existing initiatives, and potential enhancements, developing an approach that supports both immediate needs and future scalability.

  • Established an iterative design and research cadence to rapidly prototype, test, and evaluate interaction models during inception, using continuous user feedback to validate concepts and refine the experience.

Impact

  • This was one of the largest and most complex initiatives during my time at ServiceNow, collaborating with the legal and enterprise development teams.
  • Delivered critical new capabilities that addressed key customer needs, enhanced existing functionality, and expanded the value of ServiceNow's contract management experience.
  • With the help of the research team, I was able to push for innovation and a new approach in places that needed  significant improvements beyond the scope of this initiative. 
  • Enabled the CMPro and SURF engineering teams to adopt Figma as their primary design collaboration tool, while establishing a consistent UX process that became a foundation for future cross-functional collaboration and product development.
